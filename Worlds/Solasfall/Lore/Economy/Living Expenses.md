Living Expenses are the recurring cost of keeping a roof over your head in [[Solasfall]], tracked separately from [[The Weighing]]'s tax deduction. Where the Weighing is a periodic Ministry levy against your holdings, Living Expenses are the ordinary weekly cost of not sleeping in the street — rent, upkeep, and district-appropriate costs of simply existing somewhere. Failing to pay these does not trigger Ministry enforcement the way missed tax does; the consequence is losing the housing tier itself, not Debt or conscription.

## Paying Living Expenses
Cost is charged **per week** and depends on both **District** and **Housing Tier**. A character can live in a cheaper district than their nominal social standing (a Noble slumming it to save coin) or, with the right connections and enough Mints, above their apparent station, this should require a plausible in-fiction hook (a patron, a lease, a favour) rather than pure cash-on-the-barrel for the higher districts.

### Housing Tiers
| Tier | Description |
|---|---|
| **Bunk** | A shared floor, common room, or barracks-style bunk. No privacy, minimal security. |
| **Renter** | A single rented room — an inn's long-term letting rate, or a rented room in a tenement/house. |
| **Modest Housing** | A small personal apartment or house, owned or long-leased. Private, lockable, defensible. |
| **Comfortable Housing** | A well-appointed home with multiple rooms, reflecting genuine stability rather than survival. |
| **Estate** | A staffed household or noble holding — servants, grounds, and the standing that comes with them. |

### Weekly Cost by District and Tier (Gilded Mints)
| District                | Bunk | Renter | Modest | Comfortable | Estate |
| ----------------------- | ---- | ------ | ------ | ----------- | ------ |
| Slum District           | 2    | 5      | 10     | —           | —      |
| Racial Districts        | 3    | 6      | 12     | 25          | —      |
| Human Commoner District | 4    | 8      | 15     | 30          | —      |
| Entertainment District  | 5    | 12     | 22     | 45          | —      |
| Market District         | 6    | 14     | 26     | 50          | —      |
| Noble District          | —    | —      | —      | 80          | 200+   |

Dashes indicate a tier that doesn't meaningfully exist in that district — the Slum District has no Comfortable Housing to speak of, and the Noble District has nothing below Comfortable, since a rented bunk in the Noble District isn't a thing anyone offers. **Estate** cost in the Noble District is a floor, not a fixed price, actual estate upkeep should be GM-adjudicated per household based on staff, grounds, and narrative significance, similar to how [[Shop Pricing#Magical Tools|Magic Tool]] pricing above Fine quality is meant to be GM-gated rather than shelf-priced.

## Missed Payment
If a character cannot or does not pay their Living Expenses for a given week:
- **First missed week:** No immediate consequence — landlords and innkeepers generally extend short grace, though the GM may introduce a narrative complication (a pointed conversation, a locked door until payment clears).
- **Second consecutive missed week:** The character is evicted or loses access to that housing tier. They drop to the next tier down that they can currently afford, or to **Bunk** at minimum, or effectively homeless if even that isn't covered — homelessness in Solasfall should be played as a genuine narrative pressure (exposure, theft risk, social stigma) rather than a purely mechanical state.
- Re-securing a lost tier requires paying appropriate up-front costs (a deposit, GM's discretion) in addition to resuming the weekly rate.

## Interaction with The Weighing
Living Expenses and Weighing tax are calculated independently and do not offset one another — paying rent does not reduce what's owed at the Weighing, and vice versa. A player weighing whether to spend Mints on better housing versus banking savings ahead of a Weighing cycle is an intentional tension: better housing is a fixed recurring drain, while unspent holdings are a periodic proportional one. There's no universally correct answer, which is the point — it should depend on how close the next Weighing is and how flush the party currently is.
