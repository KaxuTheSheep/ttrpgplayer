## Deeper Reservoir
**Prerequisites:** None. Repeatable — each purchase increases your Essence Capacity by 1, up to a maximum of 9.
**Cost:** 1 Trait Point (2nd Essence Capacity), then 2 / 3 / 5 / 7 / 9 / 12 / 15 for each subsequent purchase up to the 9th.
Increase your Essence Capacity by 1. This node must be purchased in order — you cannot skip ahead to a later step's cost without having bought every step before it.

## Attuned Vessel
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Your [[Soul|soul]] holds an Essence's signature more comfortably. When you interchange an Essence during a Long Rest, you no longer need to have already decided which Essence to swap in before the Rest begins — you may make that choice at the end of the Rest instead.

## Steady Attunement
**Prerequisites:** Attuned Vessel.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Once per Long Rest, you may interchange an Essence during a Short Rest instead, provided you have not also attempted Self-Taught Severance, Signature Fusion, or Refined Current since your last Long Rest.

## Self-Taught Severance
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
You may attempt to sever an Essence from your own [[Soul|soul]] without a Soul Weaver, during a Long Rest. Make a **[[Attributes|Resolve]] Test (DN 16)**.
- **Success:** The Essence is permanently destroyed and removed, as though performed by a Soul Weaver. You lose **1d6 [[Sanity]]** regardless of outcome — severing a woven signature is never painless, even done correctly.
- **Failure:** The Essence remains. You lose **2d6 [[Sanity]]** instead, and gain **1 level of Exhaustion**, representing the aborted attempt tearing at the signature without completing the severance.

## Practised Hand
**Prerequisites:** Self-Taught Severance.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Reduce the DN of Self-Taught Severance to **14**, and reduce the [[Sanity]] loss on a success to **1d4**.

## Signature Fusion
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 4 Trait Points.
You may attempt to merge two Essences you currently carry into a single refined Essence, during a Long Rest. The GM determines the merged Essence's combined effect, blending both source creatures' natures into one Benefit/Drawback pair — this frees one Essence Capacity slot, since the merged result occupies only one. Make an **[[Attributes|Insight]] Test (DN 15)**.
- **Success:** The merge completes as intended.
- **Failure:** The merge still occurs, but the GM introduces a complication to the result — an unstable Drawback, an unpredictable Benefit, or a Benefit weaker than either source Essence alone. You lose **1d6 [[Sanity]]** and **1d4 [[Blood]]**, representing the physical toll of two overlapping signatures being forced into coherence.

## Refined Current
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Once per Long Rest, you may attempt to deepen an existing Essence's effect without changing its nature — strengthening its Benefit, at GM discretion for scale and appropriateness. Make a **[[Attributes|Vitality]] Test (DN 14)**.
- **Success:** The Benefit strengthens as GM-adjudicated.
- **Failure:** Nothing changes, but you still lose **4d4 [[Sanity]]** and **4d4 [[Blood]]** from the strain of pushing an already-integrated signature.

## Mastered Weaving
**Prerequisites:** Any two of Practised Hand, Signature Fusion, or Refined Current.
**Cost:** 5 Trait Points.
Choose one of Self-Taught Severance, Signature Fusion, or Refined Current that you know. That specific ability [[Sanity]] or [[Blood]] loss is halved, and its DN is reduced by a further **2**. You have not achieved a Soul Weaver's full mastery — this benefit applies to only the one ability chosen, not all three.
