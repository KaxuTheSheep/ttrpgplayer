# Tempo
![[Tempo.png]]
[[Tempo.canvas]]
## Quickened Mind
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Gain **+0.5 Actions** per Round. This bonus Half Action may only be spent on Half Action-tier activities (drawing/stowing a weapon, Jump, Quick Brace, using a Skill, simple Interact) — it cannot be used to make an Attack, cast a spell, or declare a Defensive Stance.

## Sharpened Reflex
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Gain **+1 Reaction** per Round. This bonus Reaction may only be used for **Reactive Dodge** or **Reactive Parry** — it cannot be used for a Reach Punish, Movement Interrupt, or any other Reaction type.

## Tempered Vigour
**Prerequisites:** Quickened Mind, Level 3.
**Cost:** 4 Trait Points.
Gain a further **+0.5 Actions** per Round, unrestricted — this Half Action can be spent on anything a normal Half Action could.

## Battle Instinct
**Prerequisites:** Sharpened Reflex, Level 3.
**Cost:** 4 Trait Points.
Gain a further **+1 Reaction** per Round, unrestricted — this Reaction can be used for any valid Reaction trigger.

## Unshaken Tempo
**Prerequisites:** Tempered Vigour, Level 5, 4 [[Attributes|Grace]].
**Cost:** 5 Trait Points.
Gain a further **+0.5 Actions** per Round, unrestricted. This brings your Action total to the maximum of **4**, if all prior nodes are also purchased.

## Relentless Guard
**Prerequisites:** Battle Instinct, Level 5, 4 [[Attributes|Grace]].
**Cost:** 5 Trait Points.
Gain a further **+1 Reaction** per Round, unrestricted. This brings your Reaction total to the maximum of **4**, if all prior nodes are also purchased.

## Perfect Tempo
**Prerequisites:** Unshaken Tempo, Relentless Guard. Level 7.
**Cost:** 6 Trait Points.
The restrictions on **Quickened Mind** and **Sharpened Reflex** are removed entirely — both bonuses now function as fully unrestricted Actions/Reactions. If you have not purchased both of those nodes, this has no effect until you do.
