# Ashen Follower
*All nodes require Follower status (10 Faith Points in The Ashen Crown, personal quest completed).*
![[Ashen Follower.png]]
## Blood for Blood
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
The first time each Round you take damage that reduces your Sturdiness or Blood, your next Attack this Round deals **+2 damage**.

## Iron in the Wound
**Prerequisites:** Blood for Blood.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
The bonus from Blood for Blood increases to **+4**.

## Retribution's Edge *(Incantation)*
**Prerequisites:** Iron in the Wound.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Grants the Incantation **Retribution's Edge** — as a Reaction after taking damage from a melee Attack, strike back at the attacker immediately, dealing your weapon's base damage die with no Attribute added.
**DN:** 12. **Uses per Rest:** Resolve + 1.

## Unbroken Line
**Prerequisites:** Retribution's Edge.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
While below 50% Blood, Retribution's Edge's DN is reduced by **3**.

## Vengeance Undying *(Incantation)*
**Prerequisites:** Unbroken Line.
**Cost:** 4 Trait Points.
Grants the Incantation **Vengeance Undying** — usable only as a Reaction to being hit by a Critical Success. Deal Blunt damage equal to **2d6 + Resolve** to the attacker, bypassing Sturdiness entirely.
**DN:** 14. **Uses per Rest:** Resolve.

## The Warmaiden's Answer *(Incantation)*
**Prerequisites:** Vengeance Undying.
**Cost:** 5 Trait Points.
Grants the Incantation **The Warmaiden's Answer** — usable only when an Attack would reduce you to 10% Blood or below. Before damage is applied, unleash a single devastating counter-Attack against the creature that struck you, dealing your weapon's full damage die twice, then resolve the incoming damage as normal.
**DN:** 15. **Uses per Rest:** 1 (does not scale with Resolve).

## Willing Wound
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
You may voluntarily spend your own Blood as a resource for Incantations that call for it. Spending Blood this way is a deliberate act, not damage — it does not trigger Blood Thresholds, Injuries, or Death's dying state on its own, though it can reduce you low enough that subsequent damage does.

## Tithe of Flesh *(Incantation)*
**Prerequisites:** Willing Wound.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Grants the Incantation **Tithe of Flesh** — spend up to 5 Blood as part of a declared Attack. That Attack deals bonus Fire damage equal to the Blood spent.
**DN:** 10. **Uses per Rest:** Resolve + 2.

## Bleed for Glory
**Prerequisites:** Tithe of Flesh.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
While below 50% Blood, DNs for Incantations granted by Willing Wound's line are reduced by **2**.

## Second Tithe *(Incantation)*
**Prerequisites:** Bleed for Glory.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Grants the Incantation **Second Tithe** — spend 3 Blood as a Half Action to gain **+4 Priority** to your next Action this Round.
**DN:** 11. **Uses per Rest:** Resolve + 1.

## The Debt Paid
**Prerequisites:** Second Tithe.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
The Blood cost of Tithe of Flesh and Second Tithe is reduced by **1** each (minimum 1).

## Final Offering *(Incantation)*
**Prerequisites:** The Debt Paid.
**Cost:** 5 Trait Points.
Grants the Incantation **Final Offering** — spend up to 15 Blood as a Full Action. Deal Fire damage equal to double the Blood spent to a single target within melee threat range, bypassing Armour DR entirely.
**DN:** 16. **Uses per Rest:** 1.

## Marked by Ruin
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Whenever a creature's Attack reduces you below 30% Blood, that creature becomes **Marked** by you until the end of the encounter. You know the general direction and rough distance of a Marked creature at all times.

## Legacy of Ash *(Incantation)*
**Prerequisites:** Marked by Ruin.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Grants the Incantation **Legacy of Ash** — triggers automatically, at no Action cost, the moment you fall to 0 Blood. All creatures within 3m must make a Resolve Test (DN 12) or gain Frightened toward you for 2 Rounds.
**Uses per Rest:** 1 (no roll required to activate; only the target's Resolve Test is rolled).

## The Field Remembers
**Prerequisites:** Legacy of Ash.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Allies gain **+1** to Attack Tests made against any creature you have Marked.

## Doom Echoes *(Incantation)*
**Prerequisites:** The Field Remembers.
**Cost:** 4 Trait Points.
Grants the Incantation **Doom Echoes** — as a Half Action, curse a Marked creature. If that creature is the one who reduces you to 0 Blood while the curse is active, they immediately take Fire damage equal to your maximum Blood.
**DN:** 13. **Uses per Rest:** Resolve.

## Ruinous Inheritance
**Prerequisites:** Doom Echoes.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Doom Echoes' triggered damage increases to **150%** of your maximum Blood.

## Nothing Left Unanswered *(Incantation)*
**Prerequisites:** Ruinous Inheritance.
**Cost:** 5 Trait Points.
Grants the Incantation **Nothing Left Unanswered** — triggers automatically, at no Action cost, whenever you die permanently (Exhaustion 10). Every creature that dealt damage to you during the encounter that killed you takes Fire damage equal to 25% of your maximum Blood, no Attack roll required.
**Uses per Rest:** N/A — a legacy effect, not a Rest-refreshing Incantation.

## Refuse to Fall
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
While in the dying state (0 Blood), you remain conscious and aware rather than falling unconscious. You still gain Exhaustion per Round as normal and cannot take Actions, but you may speak, observe, and use Reactions if otherwise able.

## March Everlasting *(Incantation)*
**Prerequisites:** Refuse to Fall.
**Cost:** 4 Trait Points.
Grants the Incantation **March Everlasting** — the instant an Attack would reduce you to 0 Blood, you may cast this as a Reaction. You do not enter the dying state this Round; instead you remain at 1 Blood and act normally until the start of your next turn, at which point the dying state applies retroactively unless you have been stabilised or healed above 0 in the meantime.
**DN:** 14. **Uses per Rest:** 1.

## Unyielding Steps
**Prerequisites:** March Everlasting.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
While dying, you gain Exhaustion at half the normal rate (round down, minimum 1 per 2 Rounds).

## The Ashen Stride *(Incantation)*
**Prerequisites:** Unyielding Steps.
**Cost:** 5 Trait Points.
Grants the Incantation **The Ashen Stride** — usable only while in the dying state. For 1 Round, you may act as though conscious: take a single Half Action, ignoring all Conditions imposed purely by the dying state itself (though not Conditions from other sources). You still gain Exhaustion as normal for the Round.
**DN:** 15. **Uses per Rest:** 1.

## No Rest for the Fallen
**Prerequisites:** The Ashen Stride.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
The Ashen Stride grants a **Full Action** instead of a Half Action.

## The Final March *(Incantation)*
**Prerequisites:** No Rest for the Fallen.
**Cost:** 6 Trait Points.
Grants the Incantation **The Final March** — usable only once per Long Rest, and only while dying. You return to a number of Blood equal to your Resolve score, fully conscious, and may act for up to 3 Rounds as normal. At the end of the 3rd Round, or the instant you take any further damage, you drop to 0 Blood and re-enter the dying state, gaining Exhaustion for every Round this effect was active.
**DN:** 18. **Uses per Rest:** 1 per Long Rest only.
