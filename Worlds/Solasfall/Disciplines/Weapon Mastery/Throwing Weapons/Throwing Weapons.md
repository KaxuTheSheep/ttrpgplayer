# Throwing Weapons
![[Throwing Weapons.png]]
[[Throwing Weapons.canvas]]
## Snap Draw
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
When you draw a throwing [[Weapon|weapon]] you do not currently have in hand, you may immediately make a throw [[Attacks|Attack]] with it in the same [[Ticks, Priority & Resolution|Tick]] as the draw, rather than waiting until the following [[Ticks, Priority & Resolution|Tick]].

## Twin Snap
**Prerequisites:** Snap Draw.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Snap Draw may trigger more than once per [[Rounds|Round]], provided you have a fresh throwing [[Weapon|weapon]] to draw each time.

## Fluid Grip
**Prerequisites:** Twin Snap, 3 [[Attributes|Might]] or [[Attributes|Grace]].
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Drawing a throwing [[Weapon|weapon]] via Snap Draw no longer requires an empty hand — you may discard whatever you're currently holding in that hand as part of the same free [[Actions|action]], provided you have somewhere sensible to drop or sheath it.
## Waste Not
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Throwing weapons you carry cost **1 fewer** point of [[Carrying Limits|Carrying Capacity]] each (minimum 1).

## Twist the Blade
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
If a throwing [[Weapon|weapon]] you threw is currently embedded in a creature (it hit and was not destroyed or already retrieved), you may spend a **Half [[Actions|Action]]** while adjacent to them to rip it free. This deals [[Damage Types|damage]] equal to **half the [[Weapon|weapon]]'s [[Damage Types|damage]] die (rounded down, no [[Attributes|Attribute]] added)**, of the same [[Damage Types|type]], and the [[Weapon|weapon]] returns to your hand.

## Grim Harvest
**Prerequisites:** Twist the Blade.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Twist the Blade no longer costs a separate Half [[Actions|Action]] — you may perform it as part of any melee [[Attacks|Attack Action]] you take against that creature, resolving before your [[Weapon|weapon]] [[Damage Types|damage]] is applied.

## Efficient Harvest
**Prerequisites:** Grim Harvest, Waste Not.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Once per [[Rounds|Round]], when Twist the Blade or Grim Harvest triggers, you may immediately throw the recovered [[Weapon|weapon]] at a second target within [[Range & Threat|range]] as part of the same [[Actions|Action]], at no additional cost.

## Scatter Throw
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Your throwing [[Weapon|weapon]] [[Attacks|Attack]] may instead target a cone extending out to the [[Weapon|weapon]]'s Thrown [[Range & Threat|Range]] in front of you, covering your Front Arc and one Side Arc (90° total). Make a single [[Attacks|Attack]] roll, compared individually against the [[Evasion]] of every creature in the cone. You expend **one throwing [[Weapon|weapon]] per creature hit**. [[Damage Types|Damage]] die is reduced by one step for each target hit beyond the first, to a minimum of 0.

## Ricochet
**Prerequisites:** Scatter Throw.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
A single throw (not a Scatter Throw) may strike a second creature within the [[Weapon|weapon]]'s Thrown [[Range & Threat|Range]] of the first target, provided they are roughly in line. Resolve a second [[Attacks|Attack]] roll against them using the same [[Weapon|weapon]]; only **one** [[Weapon|weapon]] is expended regardless of how many targets are struck.

## Storm of Blades
**Prerequisites:** Ricochet, 4 [[Attributes|Might]] or [[Attributes|Grace]].
**Cost:** 4 Trait Points.
Scatter Throw's [[Damage Types|damage]] penalty softens. Your [[Damage Types|damage]] die is now reduced by one step for every **two** targets hit beyond the first, rounded down, instead of every one.
## Bladed Fallback
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
You suffer no penalty switching a throwing [[Weapon|weapon]] between thrown and melee use within the same [[Rounds|Round]] — treat both as the same drawn [[Weapon|weapon]] for all purposes.

## Follow the Blade
**Prerequisites:** Bladed Fallback, Snap Draw.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Immediately after a throwing [[Weapon|weapon]] [[Attacks|Attack]], you may move up to **2m** toward the target as a free [[Actions|action]], provided you have a Half [[Actions|Action]] remaining to draw a fresh [[Weapon|weapon]] (via Snap Draw or otherwise) once you arrive.

## Wild Cast
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Throwing weapons you wield with the **Tumbling** trait have their Tumbling value increased by **+1**.

## Chaotic Momentum
**Prerequisites:** Wild Cast.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Weapons you throw with the **Volatile** trait: when resolving Volatile, you may reroll **one** of the two dice once, taking the new result.

## Master of the Throw
**Prerequisites:** Any two of Fluid Grip, Efficient Harvest, Storm of Blades, Follow the Blade, or Chaotic Momentum.
**Cost:** 5 Trait Points.
Throwing weapons you wield deal **+1 [[Damage Types|damage]] die step**. Additionally, Twist the Blade and Grim Harvest no longer cost any [[Actions|Action]] at all — they trigger as a free [[Actions|action]] whenever you're adjacent to a [[Weapon|weapon]] of yours embedded in a creature.
