# Flails & Chains
![[Flails & Chains.png]]

## Wild Momentum
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Weapons you wield with the **Unpredictable** trait have their Unpredictable value increased by **+1**.

## Erratic Follow-Through
**Prerequisites:** Wild Momentum.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Weapons you wield with the **Volatile** trait: when resolving Volatile, you may reroll **one** of the two dice once, taking the new result.

## Tempered Coil
**Prerequisites:** Wild Momentum. **Exclusive to Feral Swing.**
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
You may voluntarily reduce a wielded weapon's Unpredictable value by **2** (minimum +0), toggled freely as a free action. While reduced this way, gain **+1** to your own Parry Tests made with it.

## Feral Swing
**Prerequisites:** Wild Momentum. **Exclusive to Tempered Coil.**
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Once per [[Rounds|Round]], when you hit with a weapon that has the **Volatile** trait, you may automatically apply the higher deviation result without rolling. If you do, your next Parry Test suffers **Disadvantage**.

## Crushing Cascade
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Weapons you wield with the **Crushing** trait: when you hit a target who is currently **Blocking**, roll the Crushing die **twice** and apply both reductions.

## Reckless Chain
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
**Heavy Attacks** made with a Flails & Chains weapon suffer only **–1** to Priority instead of –2.

## Full Swing
**Prerequisites:** Reckless Chain.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Heavy Attacks made with a Flails & Chains weapon cost **1 Full Action** instead of 2. They still deal double damage as normal.

## Uncontrolled Force
**Prerequisites:** Full Swing.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Once per Round, you may forgo your ability to Parry or Dodge until the start of your next turn to add your **Might a second time** to a single Flails & Chains Heavy Attack's damage.

## Practiced Coil
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Gain **+2** to any opposed roll made during a **Weapon Bind** Disarm Attempt with a Flails & Chains weapon.

## Tightening Grip
**Prerequisites:** Practiced Coil.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
While maintaining a Bind with a Flails & Chains weapon, your Disarm Attempt no longer costs a Full Action — it costs a **Half Action** instead.

## Wrenching Pull
**Prerequisites:** Tightening Grip.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
As a **Half Action** while maintaining a Bind, force a **Might Test** (DN 10 + your Might) on the bound creature or weapon. On failure, drag the bound target or their weapon up to **2m** toward you, provided the space is unoccupied and unobstructed.

## Entangling Reach
**Prerequisites:** Practiced Coil.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Weapons you wield with the **Hooking** trait: the Might Test DN a Blocking target must beat to keep their Block active increases by **+2**.

## Snared Ground
**Prerequisites:** Entangling Reach.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Creatures currently **Grappled** or **Bound** by a Flails & Chains weapon you wield suffer **–1** to their Initiation Priority for movement taken that Round.

## Fluid Transition
**Prerequisites:** None. Requires a dual-mode weapon (e.g. Tetsu Chigaki).
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Switching a dual-mode weapon's striking end is a free action usable **once per Round** at any point during the Round, not only during the Declaration Phase.

## Twin Nature
**Prerequisites:** Fluid Transition.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Once per Round, when you attack with one end of a dual-mode weapon, your next attack this Round using the *other* end gains **Advantage**.

## Master of Coils
**Prerequisites:** Any two of Uncontrolled Force, Wrenching Pull, or Twin Nature.
**Cost:** 4 Trait Points.
While wielding a Flails & Chains weapon, treat its Unpredictable value as **2 higher** for the purpose of the target's Parry DN — even if the weapon has no Unpredictable trait innately, in which case it is treated as having Unpredictable(+2).
