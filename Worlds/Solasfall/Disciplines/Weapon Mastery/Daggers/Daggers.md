# Daggers
![[Daggers.png]]
[[Daggers.canvas]]
## Weak Point
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Gain **+1** to [[Attacks|Attack]] Tests made with a dagger against a target that is Prone, Flanked, or otherwise positionally disadvantaged.

## Anatomist's Eye
**Prerequisites:** Weak Point.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Weak Point's bonus increases to **+2**. Additionally, you always know whether a creature you can see is currently Bleeding, Poisoned, or affected by any other [[Damage Types|damage]]-over-time effect.

## Exploiting the Gap
**Prerequisites:** Anatomist's Eye.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
When you hit a target benefiting from your Weak Point bonus, you may reroll the [[Damage Types|damage]] die once and take the higher result.

## Reading the Cut
**Prerequisites:** Anatomist's Eye, Opening Cut.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Against a Bleeding target, treat them as positionally disadvantaged for the purposes of Weak Point, even if they are not actually Prone or Flanked.

## Killing Precision
**Prerequisites:** Exploiting the Gap, 3 [[Attributes|Grace]].
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
You score a Critical Success on a dagger [[Attacks|Attack]] Test on a natural **9 or 10** instead of only double 10s, provided the target is positionally disadvantaged (including via Reading the Cut).

## Opening Cut
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
On a hit with a dagger, apply **Bleeding (Rank 1)**: the target takes 1 [[Blood]] [[Damage Types|damage]] at the start of their next turn, bypassing [[Sturdiness]]. Reapplying Bleeding refreshes its duration and increases its Rank by 1 (to a maximum of Rank 3), each Rank adding +1 [[Blood]] [[Damage Types|damage]].

## Deepening Cut
**Prerequisites:** Opening Cut.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Bleeding's maximum Rank increases to **5**.

## Severing Strike
**Prerequisites:** Deepening Cut, Shadow the Blade.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Once per [[Rounds|Round]], when you hit a Bleeding target you are within [[Close-Quarters]] range of, you may trigger their Bleeding [[Damage Types|damage]] immediately instead of waiting for the start of their next turn.

## Blood Debt
**Prerequisites:** Severing Strike.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Once per [[Rounds|Round]], whenever Bleeding you inflicted deals [[Damage Types|damage]], heal **1 [[Blood]]**.

## Exsanguinate
**Prerequisites:** Blood Debt, Killing Precision.
**Cost:** 4 Trait Points.
If a Bleeding creature is reduced to 0 [[Blood]] while Bleeding, they gain **2 Exhaustion** from that instance instead of the normal 1. Additionally, Killing Precision's expanded critical [[Damage Types|damage]] range also applies against any Bleeding target, regardless of positional disadvantage.

## Snap Thrust
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Once per [[Rounds|Round]], as part of the same Full [[Actions|Action]] as a dagger [[Attacks|Attack]], make one additional dagger [[Attacks|Attack]] at **–3**.

## Quickened Strikes
**Prerequisites:** Snap Thrust, 2 [[Attributes|Grace]].
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Snap Thrust's penalty reduces to **–1**.

## Relentless Point
**Prerequisites:** Quickened Strikes.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
[[Action Fatigue]] no longer accumulates on dagger [[Attacks|Attacks]] used in consecutive [[Rounds]].

## Feinting Step
**Prerequisites:** Quickened Strikes, Inside the Guard.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Once per [[Rounds|Round]], Snap Thrust's extra [[Attacks|Attack]] may instead target a *different* creature than your first [[Attacks|Attack]] this [[Rounds|Round]], provided both are within [[Close-Quarters]] range of you.

## Blur of Steel
**Prerequisites:** Relentless Point, 4 [[Attributes|Grace]].
**Cost:** 4 Trait Points.
Snap Thrust's extra [[Attacks|Attack]] now costs a **Half [[Actions|Action]]** instead of riding on the first [[Attacks|Attack]]'s Full [[Actions|Action]], and may be used any time you've made a dagger [[Attacks|Attack]] this [[Rounds|Round]].

## Inside the Guard
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Gain **Advantage** on dagger [[Attacks|Attack]] Tests against creatures wielding a Reach [[Weapon|weapon]], on top of [[Close-Quarters]] Proficiency's normal benefit.

## Slip the Grapple
**Prerequisites:** Inside the Guard.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
You no longer suffer Disadvantage on dagger [[Attacks|Attacks]] while Grappled or Grappling.

## Shadow the Blade
**Prerequisites:** Slip the Grapple, 3 [[Attributes|Grace]].
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Once per [[Rounds|Round]], when you hit with a dagger, shift **1m** to an adjacent unoccupied space as a free [[Actions|action]], provided you remain within [[Close-Quarters]] range of the target.

## Predator's Reposition
**Prerequisites:** Shadow the Blade, Quickened Strikes.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Shadow the Blade's shift increases to **2m** and no longer provokes a Reach Punish.


## Twin Fangs
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
While dual-wielding daggers, your Off-Hand Strike resolves at **–1** of your main-hand [[Weapon|weapon]]'s [[Ticks, Priority & Resolution|Priority]] instead of –2.

## Mirrored Strikes
**Prerequisites:** Twin Fangs.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Your Off-Hand Strike may apply this tree's on-hit effects (e.g. Opening Cut's Bleeding), rather than being restricted to plain [[Weapon|weapon]] die [[Damage Types|damage]] only.

## Twin Precision
**Prerequisites:** Mirrored Strikes, Weak Point.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Your Off-Hand Strike also benefits from Weak Point's bonus (and any upgrades to it) against qualifying targets.

## Whirling Fangs
**Prerequisites:** Mirrored Strikes, 4 [[Attributes|Grace]].
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Once per [[Rounds|Round]], make a **second** Off-Hand Strike as part of the same Full [[Actions|Action]], at the same –1 [[Ticks, Priority & Resolution|Priority]] reduction, provided you are dual-wielding daggers.

## Master of Knives
**Prerequisites:** Any two of Killing Precision, Exsanguinate, Blur of Steel, Predator's Reposition, or Whirling Fangs.
**Cost:** 5 Trait Points.
Daggers you wield deal **+1 die step** (1d4 → 1d6), and you gain **+1** to all [[Attacks|Attack]] Tests made with a dagger.
