# Viper
![[Viper.png]]
## Practiced Application
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Coating a [[Weapon|weapon]] with a toxin no longer costs a Half [[Actions|Action]] — it becomes a **free [[Actions|action]]**, once per [[Rounds|Round]].

**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Unused applications on a coated [[Weapon|weapon]] no longer degrade after combat ends — a coated blade stays viable until its applications are spent, however much time passes.

## Efficient Dosage
**Prerequisites:** Practiced Application.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Your technique wastes less of each vial. A toxin vial now provides **15 applications** instead of 10.

## Twin Coat
**Prerequisites:** Efficient Dosage.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
You may coat **two** [[Weapon|weapons]] (or one [[Weapon|weapon]] and your [[Off-Hand & Dual-Wielding|off-hand]] [[Weapon|weapon]]) as a single free [[Actions|action]] via Practiced Application, consuming applications from each vial independently.

## Concentrated Dose
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
You understand precisely how much a body can be pushed before it resists. Increase the DN of any resist Test a toxin you apply forces by **+2**.

## Preferred Method
**Prerequisites:** Concentrated Dose.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Your understanding of a toxin's chemistry lets you compensate for imperfect delivery. Toxins you apply are always treated as delivered via their **Preferred Application** (see [[Toxins]]) for the purposes of the +3 DN bonus, regardless of the actual method used.

## Lethal Refinement
**Prerequisites:** Preferred Method.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
[[Damage Types|Damage]]-over-time effects from toxins you apply deal **+1** per [[Rounds|Round]], and duration-based effects (Chill, Stun, Disadvantage windows, and similar) last **1 additional [[Rounds|Round]]**.

## Overdose
**Prerequisites:** Lethal Refinement, Efficient Dosage.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Once per [[Rest & Recovery|Rest]], you may apply a toxin at double concentration as part of a single [[Attacks|Attack]], consuming **2 applications** at once. The resist DN increases by an additional **+3** on top of Concentrated Dose, and any [[Damage Types|damage]]-over-time effect rolls twice, taking the higher result, for its full duration.

## Swift Sting
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Switching which coated [[Weapon|weapon]] you are wielding no longer risks wasting the [[Attacks|Attack]] — you may draw a pre-coated [[Weapon|weapon]] and [[Attacks|Attack]] with it in the same [[Ticks, Priority & Resolution|Tick]], provided [[Drawing & Stowing|drawing]] it would normally be a Half [[Actions|Action]].

## Double Bite
**Prerequisites:** Swift Sting, Twin Coat.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Once per [[Rounds|Round]], if you hit the same target twice with coated [[Weapon|weapons]] in a single [[Rounds|Round]] (via [[Off-Hand & Dual-Wielding|dual-wielding]], a Full [[Actions|Action]] granting two [[Attacks|Attacks]], or similar), the second application's resist Test is made at **Disadvantage**, reflecting a body already straining against the first dose.

## Viper's Kiss
**Prerequisites:** Double Bite, Overdose.
**Cost:** 4 Trait Points.
Once per [[Rounds|Round]], when a toxin you've applied would deal its [[Damage Types|damage]]-over-time [[Damage Types|damage]], you may cause it to bypass [[Sturdiness]] entirely, applying directly to [[Blood]] as though freshly injected.

## Iron Stomach
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Gain **Advantage** on [[Attributes|Vitality]] Tests made to resist toxins.

## Tempered Body
**Prerequisites:** Iron Stomach. 
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points. 
You gain a new rest activity, Toxin Immunity, you actively administer specific toxin via the application method in small doses, you roll a vitality check against the DN of the specific toxin, if you success you build up 1 immunity point, if you fail the dosage is small enough were the toxin is docile. Every Immunity point gives you +1 to the roll against that specific toxin.

## Toxin Blood
**Prerequisites:** Tempered Body, 4 [[Attributes|Vitality]]. 
**Cost:** 4 Trait Points.
Now, when you reach 5 immunity points against a specific toxin, the effects of it are halved in all senses, (duration, damage, etc). At 10 points, you are fully immune to that toxin, any time you have been administered a quantity of toxin that would cause a roll, if it is one of the toxins you built immunity to you auto succeed it.

## Purging Focus
**Prerequisites:** Tempered Body, Concentrated Dose.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Understanding exactly how a toxin works lets you fight it on your own terms. As a **Half [[Actions|Action]]**, you may attempt an early [[Attributes|Vitality]] Test against an active toxin's resist DN, even if the toxin's own rules don't normally allow a Test until the next [[Rounds|Round]] or trigger point.

## Antidote Blood
**Prerequisites:** Toxin Blood.
**Cost:** 4 Trait Points.
The immunity from toxins in your blood created a Mithridate, an all in 1 antidote against those toxins. You deal 2 blood damage to yourself and give the Mithridate to someone to cure them from the toxin of normal dosage if its one of the toxins you have built immunity towards. For higher dosages, you may have to deal more damage to yourself for the effort to be nullified.

## Practiced Harvester
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
You may attempt to extract usable venom from a freshly killed venomous creature via a **[[Skills|Crafting]] Test** (DN set by the GM based on the creature), yielding vial applications appropriate to that creature's natural toxin rather than needing to purchase or synthesise one.

## Keen Nose
**Prerequisites:** Practiced Harvester.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Gain **Advantage** on [[Attributes|Insight]] Tests made to identify a toxin by scent, residue, or symptom — whether on a coated [[Weapon|weapon]], a poisoned drink, or an already-affected creature.

## Alchemist's Economy
**Prerequisites:** Keen Nose, Efficient Dosage.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Your understanding of toxin chemistry lets you stretch and stabilise your supply. Reduce the Gilded Mint cost of any toxin or antidote you purchase by **25%**, and halve the time required to harvest venom via Practiced Harvester.

## Master of Venom
**Prerequisites:** Any two of Viper's Kiss, Antidote Blood, Alchemist's Economy, or Overdose.
**Cost:** 5 Trait Points.
Once per encounter, applying a toxin costs **no application charge** from its vial — your precision has become good enough that a single dose stretches further than it should. Additionally, your immunity from Tempered Body extends to **any** toxin you can correctly identify via Keen Nose before exposure, not only ones you crafted or applied yourself.
