# Deceiver
![[Deceiver.png]]
[[Deceiver.canvas]]
## Convincing Guise
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Gain **+1** to your [[Disguise]] Test (the [[Skills|Performance]] roll that sets your Disguise DN).

## Practiced Persona
**Prerequisites:** Convincing Guise.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
The bonus from Convincing Guise increases to **+2** total.

## Seamless Character
**Prerequisites:** Practiced Persona.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Once per [[Rest & Recovery|Rest]], when you would break character in a way that risks your [[Disguise]], you may make a [[Skills|Performance]] Test (DN set by the GM based on the severity of the slip) instead of immediately provoking an [[Attributes|Insight]] Test from observers. On success, the moment passes unremarked.

## Master of Faces
**Prerequisites:** Seamless Character.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
You may maintain **two** separate [[Disguise|Disguised]] identities to different groups of observers simultaneously, provided those groups have no reason to compare notes on you. If they do compare notes, both identities are immediately at risk per the GM's discretion.

## Practiced Listener
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Gain **+1** to [[Attributes|Insight]] Tests made to eavesdrop, follow multiple conversations at once, or pick up incidental information you weren't meant to hear.

## Silver Ear
**Prerequisites:** Practiced Listener.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
The bonus from Practiced Listener increases to **+2** total. Additionally, while [[Stealth|Hidden]] from a creature, you may eavesdrop on them without an additional Test beyond maintaining Hidden itself.

## Cold Read
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
The first time you meet a creature, you may spend a **Half [[Actions|Action]]** to make an [[Attributes|Insight]] Test (DN set by the GM) to read them at a glance — learning one true detail about their current emotional state, allegiance, or recent circumstance.

## Practiced Read
**Prerequisites:** Cold Read.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Cold Read may now also be attempted against creatures you have met before but have not read this way recently (GM discretion on cooldown), and reveals **two** details instead of one on a success.

## Silver Approach
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Gain **+1** to [[Skills|Negotiation]] Tests made to charm, flatter, or otherwise manipulate someone into trust or favour.

## Practiced Charm
**Prerequisites:** Silver Approach.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
The bonus from Silver Approach increases to **+2** total.

## Manufactured Trust
**Prerequisites:** Practiced Charm.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Once per [[Rest & Recovery|Rest]], treat a failed [[Skills|Negotiation]] Test made to build rapport or trust as a success, provided the margin of failure was **3 or less**.

## Long Con
**Prerequisites:** Manufactured Trust.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
When you invest meaningful downtime cultivating a false identity or relationship with a specific NPC across multiple sessions, you gain **Advantage** on all further [[Skills|Negotiation]] Tests made against them for as long as the con holds, at GM discretion for how much time and consistency this requires.

## Deflected Suspicion
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
When accused or suspected of something, you may make a [[Skills|Negotiation]] Test to redirect suspicion toward a plausible third party instead of denying the accusation outright. The GM adjudicates plausibility based on the fiction.

## Practiced Alibi
**Prerequisites:** Deflected Suspicion.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
If you establish an alibi in advance of the moment it's needed, gain **Advantage** on the Deflected Suspicion Test when the time comes to use it.

## Scapegoat
**Prerequisites:** Practiced Alibi.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Once per session, a successful Deflected Suspicion Test fully clears you of suspicion in the eyes of that observer for the remainder of the scene, rather than merely casting doubt.

## Vanishing Trail
**Prerequisites:** Scapegoat.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Immediately after a successful Deflected Suspicion or Scapegoat Test, your actual movements and actions for that scene become untraceable to ordinary investigation — [[Skills|Investigation]] Tests made to retrace your true steps suffer **Disadvantage**.

## Master of Masks
**Prerequisites:** Any two of Master of Faces, Long Con, Scapegoat, or Vanishing Trail.
**Cost:** 5 Trait Points.
Once per [[Rest & Recovery|Rest]], you may declare that a Disguise, con, or alibi you are currently maintaining has, in fact, been airtight the entire time — retroactively negate one failed Test made against you this session related to that specific deception (Disguise detection, Negotiation, or Investigation), as though it had succeeded. The GM determines how this is narrated, but it cannot undo consequences that have already meaningfully changed the story (a body already found, a fight already started).
