# Assassin
![[Assassin.png]]

## Opening the Guard
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
[[Attacks|Attacks]] you make against a creature that has not detected you (see [[Stealth]]) also ignore **[[Block]]**, in addition to the Dodge and Parry denial Stealth already grants.

## Severing Blow
**Prerequisites:** Opening the Guard, Level 3.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Such [[Attacks|Attacks]] also bypass **[[Sturdiness]]** entirely, applying their [[Damage Types|damage]] directly to [[Blood]].

## Perfect Opening
**Prerequisites:** Severing Blow, Level 5, 4 [[Attributes|Grace]] or [[Attributes|Might]].
**Cost:** 4 Trait Points.
You score a Critical Success on such [[Attacks|Attacks]] on a natural **9 or 10** instead of only double 10s.

## Executioner's Eye
**Prerequisites:** Level 2.
**Cost:** 4 Trait Points.
Once per [[Rounds|Round]], if a creature's current [[Blood]] is at or below **2** and you are currently [[Stealth|Hidden]] from them or they have not otherwise detected you, any [[Attacks|Attack]] that lands against them instead reduces them to **0 [[Blood]]** outright — bypassing [[Sturdiness]] and [[Solasfall Armour|Armour]] DR entirely for this purpose.

## Practiced Execution
**Prerequisites:** Executioner's Eye, Level 3.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
The threshold increases to **5**.

## Merciless Precision
**Prerequisites:** Practiced Execution, Level 3.
**Cost:** 4 Trait Points.
The threshold increases to **8**.

## Reaper's Judgment
**Prerequisites:** Merciless Precision, Level 5, 4 [[Attributes|Might]] or [[Attributes|Grace]].
**Cost:** 5 Trait Points.
The threshold increases to **12**, and the once-per-[[Rounds|Round]] limit is removed — it triggers on every qualifying hit.

## Marked for Death
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
As a **Half [[Actions|Action]]**, declare a single visible creature as your **Mark**. While a Mark is active, you cannot willingly target any other creature with an [[Attacks|Attack]]. Ending this early requires **denouncing** the Mark as a Half [[Actions|Action]]; it also ends automatically if the Mark is reduced to 0 [[Blood]]. While Marked, gain **+1** to [[Attacks|Attack]] Tests against them.

## Focused Malice
**Prerequisites:** Marked for Death.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
The bonus increases to **+2**. The first time each encounter you hit your Mark, reroll the [[Damage Types|damage]] die once and take the higher result.

## Undivided Purpose
**Prerequisites:** Focused Malice, Level 3.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
While you have an active Mark, gain **Advantage** on Tests made to track, follow, or relocate them, and their attempts to hide specifically from you suffer **Disadvantage**.

## Unrelenting
**Prerequisites:** Undivided Purpose, Level 5, 4 [[Attributes|Grace]] or [[Attributes|Might]].
**Cost:** 4 Trait Points.
Once per encounter, if your Mark would otherwise break line of sight or evade your detection, you may immediately move up to your Run Travel Rate for free as a **Reaction** to close the distance.

## Full Commitment
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Once per [[Rounds|Round]], you may forgo your ability to Parry, Dodge, or Block until the start of your next turn to add your **[[Attributes|Grace]] or [[Attributes|Might]]** a second time to a single [[Attacks|Attack]]'s [[Damage Types|damage]].

## Killing Intent
**Prerequisites:** Full Commitment, Level 3.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
When Full Commitment is used against a target that has not detected you, also add **one additional [[Weapon|weapon]] [[Damage Types|damage]] die**.

## No Second Chances
**Prerequisites:** Killing Intent, Level 5, 5 [[Attributes|Might]] or [[Attributes|Grace]].
**Cost:** 5 Trait Points.
Once per [[Rest & Recovery|Rest]], an [[Attacks|Attack]] empowered by Full Commitment against an undetecting target is treated as an automatic **Critical Success**, rolling on the Critical Success Table in addition to its normal [[Damage Types|damage]].

## Momentum of the Kill
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Once per [[Rounds|Round]], if an [[Attacks|Attack]] you make reduces a creature to 0 [[Blood]] or triggers an execute, you may immediately move up to half your Walk Travel Rate as a free [[Actions|action]].

## Second Target
**Prerequisites:** Momentum of the Kill, Level 3.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
The first time each [[Rounds|Round]] you reduce a creature to 0 [[Blood]] or trigger an execute, your next [[Attacks|Attack]] this [[Rounds|Round]] against a **different** creature is made with **Advantage**.

## Silent Finish
**Prerequisites:** Second Target, Level 5, 4 [[Attributes|Grace]] or [[Attributes|Insight]].
**Cost:** 4 Trait Points.
Instead of dealing [[Damage Types|damage]], an [[Attacks|Attack]] that would qualify for Momentum of the Kill's trigger against a target that has not detected you may instead render them **Unconscious** rather than killing them, at your choice, without needing to overcome [[Sturdiness]] first.

## The Perfect Kill
**Prerequisites:** Any two of Perfect Opening, Reaper's Judgment, Unrelenting, No Second Chances, or Silent Finish. Level 8.
**Cost:** 6 Trait Points.
[[Attributes|Grace]] per [[Rest & Recovery|Rest]], when you hit a creature that has not detected you, you may deal (15% of their blood + 5% per 20% missing) as damage straight to their blood, this may only trigger once per target.
