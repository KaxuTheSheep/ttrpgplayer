# Infiltrator
![[Infiltrator.png]]

## Light Feet
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
You no longer suffer Disadvantage on [[Skills|Stealth]] Tests from moving across noisy or difficult terrain, provided you remain at Walk pace or slower.

## Stealth Run
**Prerequisites:** Light Feet.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Once per [[Rounds|Round]], you may Run without automatically ending [[Stealth|Hidden]] — instead, make a [[Skills|Stealth]] Test as normal. Failure ends Hidden as it normally would for [[Movement Types|Running]].

## Practiced Flight
**Prerequisites:** Stealth Run, 3 [[Attributes|Grace]] or [[Attributes|Insight]].
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Stealth Run's Test is made with **Advantage**.

## Ghost Stride
**Prerequisites:** Practiced Flight.
**Cost:** 4 Trait Points.
You may Run while [[Stealth|Hidden]] with no Test required at all, provided no observer has detected you so far this [[Rounds|Round]].

## Battlefield Confusion
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
As a **Full [[Actions|Action]]**, if an observer trying to detect you is currently engaged in melee (within [[Close-Quarters]] range) with an ally of yours, you may attempt to become [[Stealth|Hidden]] from them despite their attention, using the fighting as cover. Make a [[Skills|Stealth]] Test at **Disadvantage**.

## Practiced Disengage
**Prerequisites:** Battlefield Confusion, Stealth Run.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Battlefield Confusion's Disadvantage is removed, provided the ally remains engaged with the observer.

## Vanish in the Fray
**Prerequisites:** Practiced Disengage.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
You no longer need a specific ally engaged — any active combat (allies or otherwise) occurring within the observer's [[Close-Quarters]] range is sufficient cover.

## Slip Away
**Prerequisites:** Vanish in the Fray, 4 [[Attributes|Grace]] or [[Attributes|Insight]].
**Cost:** 4 Trait Points.
You may attempt this as a **Half [[Actions|Action]]** instead of a Full [[Actions|Action]].

## Practiced Concealment
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Gain **+1** to [[Skills|Stealth]] Tests.

## Deepened Concealment
**Prerequisites:** Practiced Concealment.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
The bonus increases to **+2** total.

## Steady Nerve
**Prerequisites:** Deepened Concealment.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
If a [[Skills|Perception]] Test against your Stealth DN succeeds by a margin of **2 or less**, the observer isn't certain of what they saw. They must make an [[Attributes|Insight]] Test (DN equal to your original [[Skills|Stealth]] Test result) to actually confirm your detection. On failure, you remain [[Stealth|Hidden]] against them.

## Unshaken Composure
**Prerequisites:** Steady Nerve, 3 [[Attributes|Grace]] or [[Attributes|Insight]].
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
While [[Stealth|Hidden]], being caught in an area effect or environmental hazard that does not itself require an [[Attacks|Attack]] roll does not automatically reveal you, nor does it force a [[Skills|Perception]] Test against you.

## Deft Fingers
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Gain **+1 training** in [[Skills|Deft Hands]], specifically for lockpicking and disarming mechanical traps. This may exceed your linked [[Attributes|Attribute]] cap by 1.

## Trap Sense
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Gain **Advantage** on [[Skills|Perception]] Tests made to detect traps, tripwires, or hidden mechanisms.

## Practiced Hands
**Prerequisites:** Deft Fingers.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Halve the time required to pick a lock or disarm a trap.

## Silent Bypass
**Prerequisites:** Practiced Hands, Trap Sense, Steady Nerve.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Picking locks or disarming traps no longer risks alerting nearby creatures — doing so does not require a [[Skills|Stealth]] Test to remain [[Stealth|Hidden]], nor can it be heard by anyone outside your immediate reach.

## Ghost of the Battlefield
**Prerequisites:** Any two of Ghost Stride, Slip Away, Unshaken Composure, or Silent Bypass.
**Cost:** 5 Trait Points.
You may attempt to re-enter [[Stealth|Hidden]] (per the core Stealth rules) as a **Half [[Actions|Action]]** instead of a Full [[Actions|Action]], and without the normal Disadvantage that re-entering imposes.
