# Spellslinger
![[Spellslinger.png]]
[[Sigilwright.canvas]]
## Battle Focus
**Prerequisites:** 2 Might or Grace.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Gain **+1 Priority** to Flux spells cast on the same turn you also make a weapon Attack (melee or ranged) — the two must occur within the same Round.

## Effortless Spell
**Prerequisites:** Battle Focus.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Your need for low-effort spells in the heat of combat has trained you to shape magic more economically. Enhancement Points invested in a Flux spell you cast now increase the reduction to its Action Cost by an additional **0.5** per point.

## Practiced Alternation
**Prerequisites:** 3 Might or Grace.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Once per Round, after making a weapon Attack, your next Flux spell cast that same Round has its Control DN reduced by **2**.

## Answering Cast
**Prerequisites:** Practiced Alternation.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Practiced Alternation now also works in reverse — after casting a Flux spell, your next weapon Attack that same Round gains **+1** to its Attack Test.

## Steady Hands
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
While wielding a weapon in at least one hand, gain **+1** to Flux Casting rolls (2d10 + Insight).

## Split Focus
**Prerequisites:** Steady Hands, 4 Might or Grace.
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
You may declare a **Defensive Stance** (Block, Parry, or Dodge) and still cast a Flux spell the same Round, provided the spell's Action Cost is 1 Action or less. Casting under a declared stance does not end it.

## Momentum Casting
**Prerequisites:** Effortless Spell, Answering Cast.
**Cost:** 4 Trait Points.
Once per Round, if you have both made a weapon Attack and cast a Flux spell this Round, gain **+2 Priority** to whichever of the two resolves second.

## Unbroken Cadence
**Prerequisites:** Momentum Casting, Split Focus.
**Cost:** 5 Trait Points.
The Round-based restriction ("once per Round") on Battle Focus, Practiced Alternation, and Momentum Casting is removed for you — their effects trigger every time their condition is met, not just the first.
