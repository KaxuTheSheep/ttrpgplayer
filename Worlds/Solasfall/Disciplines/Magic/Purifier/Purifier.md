# Purifier
![[Purifier.png]]
## Discordant Presence
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Your natural resistance to foreign mana radiates faintly outward. Gain **+1** to any Insight Test made to detect, identify, or disrupt a magical effect — including Mana Disruption's opposed roll and any spell identification Tests you have access to elsewhere.

## Attuned Disruption
**Prerequisites:** Discordant Presence.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
The bonus from Discordant Presence increases to **+2** total.

## Mana Disruption
**Prerequisites:** None.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
When you witness a creature within line of sight begin casting a Flux spell, you may spend your Reaction to attempt disruption. Make an opposed Insight Test against the caster's Insight + the spell's Control DN. On success, their Control DN for that cast increases by **+4**. This can cause a successful cast to become a Partial Success or Backlash.

## Disruptive Channel
**Prerequisites:** Mana Disruption.
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
Your Mana Disruption can now target:
- **Any spell being cast** (not just Flux)
- **Any spell already active** (weaken its structure — spend an Action, make the opposed Test, and on success reduce its duration by 1 round or reduce its effect by half)

## Circle Breaker
**Prerequisites:** None. Does not require casting training — see [[Requirements]].
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
You've learned exactly where a Weave circle's structure is weakest. As part of a melee Attack that hits a surface bearing an active or still-forming Weave circle — inscribed stone, magic ink, a scroll, or similar — you may declare Circle Breaker instead of dealing your weapon's normal damage. The circle's physical medium is disrupted: a Woven effect ends immediately, and a Drawn Weave mid-construction suffers **2 instances of Disadvantage** on its next Defect Test, in addition to any Defect Tests it would normally require.

## Marked Geometry
**Prerequisites:** Circle Breaker. Does not require casting training — see [[Requirements]].
**Cost:** 2 Trait Points.
You recognise the seams of a Weave circle even while it's still being drawn. You have **Advantage** on Attack Tests made against a creature currently mid-draw on a Drawn Weave.

## Shattered Casting
**Prerequisites:** Circle Breaker. Does not require casting training — see [[Requirements]].
**Cost:** 3 Trait Points.
Circle Breaker no longer requires a melee Attack. As a **Half Action**, you may target a Weave circle or its inscribed surface within your weapon's range (thrown or ranged) with an Attack roll against a static DN of 12, applying Circle Breaker's disruption on a hit without needing to strike the caster directly.

## Spell Contest
**Prerequisites:** Disruptive Channel, Marked Geometry.
**Cost:** 5 Trait Points.
When you successfully disrupt a Flux spell using Mana Disruption, or successfully apply Circle Breaker to a Weave circle before it completes, you may choose to enter a **Spell Contest** instead of the normal disruption effect. Make an opposed Insight Test against the caster: on success, you seize control of the spell or circle — it resolves immediately using your chosen target and variables (within its existing Enhancement limits). On failure, the spell or circle resolves normally against you, and you take a **–2 Priority** penalty to your next Action as feedback rattles your concentration.
