# Magic Discipline Requirements

Unless a node explicitly states otherwise, taking **any** node within the Magic Disciplines (Conduit, Sigilwright, Architect, Purifier, and any future addition to this category) requires that your character already knows how to cast [[magic]] — you must be a trained conductor capable of shaping [[Mana|mana]] through your [[Soul|soul]], via either Flux or Weave Casting. A character with no casting training cannot purchase these nodes, regardless of Trait Points available or prerequisites otherwise met, in the same way Disciplines as a whole are hard-locked at level 0 for everyone.

This reflects that most of what these trees improve — casting precision, Enhancement scaling, Defect Tests, spell identification through lived experience of the process — assumes a caster's perspective. You cannot refine a skill you have never practised.

## Exceptions
The following nodes, all within **[[Purifier]]**, are explicitly exempt from this requirement. They represent physical and theoretical understanding of how a Weave circle *works* rather than the ability to conduct [[mana]] through one — the same way a sapper doesn't need to be an architect to know where a wall will collapse.

- **Circle Breaker**
- **Marked Geometry**
- **Shattered Casting**

A character without any casting training may purchase these three nodes freely, subject to their normal prerequisites and Trait Point costs. All other Purifier nodes — including Discordant Presence, Attuned Disruption, Mana Disruption, Disruptive Channel, and Spell Contest — still require casting training as normal, since they hinge on sensing or contesting [[mana]] directly rather than recognising physical circle structure.
