Weapon Construction covers the classification categories every [[Weapon|weapon]] is built from — handedness and weight class. Unlike [[Weapon Traits (Universal)|Weapon Traits]], these aren't optional add-ons a weapon may or may not have; every weapon has exactly one Handedness and exactly one Weight Class, and these categories shape how other rules (Carrying Limits, Off-Hand & Dual-Wielding, Dodge) apply to that weapon.

## Handedness

### One-Handed
- Can be wielded with one hand.
- May be paired with a **shield**, a **second [[Weapon|weapon]]**, or left-hand **grappling**.
- Can be switched between hands as a **free [[Actions|action]]**.

### Two-Handed
- Designed to be used with **both hands** to maintain leverage, stability, and edge alignment.
- The wielder may **momentarily release one hand** to perform **[[Parry]]**, **Grapple**, or **minor interactions** **without changing stance**.
  The [[Weapon|weapon]] is still considered **Two-Handed** for all mechanical purposes.

### Versatile
- Can be wielded with **one** or **two** hands.
- When wielded with two hands, increase the [[Damage Types|damage]] die by one step (d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 → d14).

## Weight Class

### Heavy
- Requires committed body [[Movement Types|movement]] to use effectively.
- Reduce the [[Dodge]] [[Movement Types|distance]] by 1m.

### Standard
- No effect.

### Light
- Easy to manoeuvre in tight space.
- Can be used while **[[Dodge]]** is active without the disadvantage penalty.
- Can be **dual-wielded**.

> Weight Class here governs combat handling. For how Weight Class affects what a character can carry, see [[Carrying Limits]] — the two use the same category names but answer different questions (handling vs load).

### Thrown
This [[Weapon|weapon]] is designed to be hurled at [[Range & Threat|range]]. Thrown weapons resolve at their Initiation [[Ticks, Priority & Resolution|Priority]] — the projectile travels its full [[Range & Threat|range]] in a single [[Ticks, Priority & Resolution|Priority]] instance and does not continue into subsequent steps. Any creature or object in the path is a valid hit candidate, resolved nearest first.

**Retrieval:**
- Thrown weapons that miss land at their maximum [[Range & Threat|range]] or at the first obstruction along their path. Moving to their location is sufficient to retrieve them — no [[Actions|action]] cost.
- Thrown weapons that hit an unarmoured or lightly armoured target remain retrievable. Moving adjacent to a downed target is sufficient to retrieve them — no [[Actions|action]] cost.
- Thrown weapons that hit a heavily armoured target are not automatically destroyed but may be damaged or awkwardly lodged at GM discretion.
- Thrown weapons are only destroyed on a Critical Failure or in extreme circumstances at GM discretion (striking solid stone, structural impacts, etc).

**Retrieval:**
- Ammunition that misses is recoverable after combat unless the GM determines the environment makes retrieval impossible or the ammunition itself is volatile.
- Ammunition that strikes a heavily armoured target is destroyed on impact.
- Ammunition that strikes an unarmoured or lightly armoured target is recoverable after combat. Moving adjacent to a downed target is sufficient to retrieve it — no [[Actions|action]] cost.
