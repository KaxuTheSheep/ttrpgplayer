Weapons define how a character can fight in combat. Each [[Weapon|weapon]] provides its own balance of reach, [[Ticks, Priority & Resolution|Priority]], power, and defensive interaction. Choosing a weapon is choosing a fighting style.

This page is a hub — it explains what a weapon *is* and points to where the mechanical detail lives. It carries no stat template and no trait list of its own.

## Shared Properties
All weapons, regardless of delivery method, share the following properties:

**[[Damage Types|Damage]] Die:** The base [[Damage Types|damage]] the weapon inflicts before adding [[Attributes|Attribute]]

**[[Ticks, Priority & Resolution|Priority]] (Base):** Determines how quickly the weapon can be brought to bear. Higher Priority resolves earlier in the [[Rounds|round]].

**[[Range & Threat|Range]]:** How far from your body the weapon threatens space, or how far it can be delivered — the exact field differs by template (see below).

**Handedness:** One-Handed, Two-Handed, or Versatile.

**Weight Class:** Light, Standard, or Heavy.

**Traits:** Additional rules that modify how the weapon interacts with combat.

## Delivery Templates
A weapon is built from one of three templates, depending on how it reaches its target. See [[Weapon Construction]] for the full field schema of each:
- **Melee** — threatens space directly around the wielder.
- **Thrown** — hurled at range, resolves in a single Priority instance.
- **Ranged** — fired at range, resolves as a tracked or instant projectile per [[Ranged Attacks]].

[[Weapon Construction]] also covers Handedness and Weight Class in full, including how each affects handling.

## Traits
Traits are optional rules layered onto a weapon on top of its template. Two documents hold them:
- [[Weapon Traits]] — usable by any setting built on Core.
- A setting's own traits document (e.g. [[Solasfall Weapon Traits]]) — traits that depend on a setting-specific mechanic.

## Handling Weapons in Combat
For rules on drawing, stowing, and switching weapons mid-combat, see [[Drawing & Stowing]]. For dual-wielding and off-hand strikes, see [[Off-Hand & Dual-Wielding]].
