Stealth represents a creature's ability to avoid detection through concealment, stillness, and controlled [[Movement Types|movement]]. It produces the **Hidden** [[Conditions|condition]], tracked individually per observer — you may be Hidden from one creature and detected by another simultaneously.

## Becoming Hidden
You may attempt to become Hidden if you have some form of concealment from a given observer: standing in their Blurry or Black Zone (see [[Vision]]), behind physical cover, obscured by smoke or disguise, or simply outside their line of sight.

Make a **[[Skills|Stealth]] Test**. The result becomes your **Stealth DN** against that observer until something ends it (below).

## Detection
Any observer who could plausibly notice you may attempt a **[[Skills|Perception]] Test** against your Stealth DN, either on their own turn or at GM discretion for passive awareness (a patrolling guard's routine check, for instance).
- **Success:** that observer detects you. Hidden ends *for them specifically* — others who haven't rolled, or who failed, may still be unaware.
- **Failure:** they remain unaware.

## Maintaining Hidden
- [[Movement Types|Walking]] or slower does not require a new Test, provided you don't move into an observer's Clear Zone while they'd have a plain look at you (GM discretion).
- **[[Movement Types|Running]]** immediately ends Hidden against all observers — no Test, it is simply incompatible with careful [[Movement Types|movement]].
- Silent [[Weapon|weapons]], quiet terrain, or similar factors may grant Advantage on the Test at GM discretion.

## Attacking from Hidden
Making an [[Attacks|Attack]] against a creature that has not detected you treats that creature as if you were attacking from their **Black Zone** (see [[Vision]]):
- You gain **Advantage** on the [[Attacks|Attack]] roll.
- The target cannot use **[[Dodge]]** or **[[Parry]]** against it.

Making the [[Attacks|Attack]] ends Hidden for you against that target (and typically against anyone else who witnessed it), unless a specific ability states otherwise.

## Re-entering Hidden
If you break line of sight with every observer who has detected you — ducking around cover, into darkness, through a door — and spend a Full [[Actions|Action]] doing so without attacking, you may attempt a new [[Skills|Stealth]] Test against those observers at **Disadvantage**, reflecting that they know roughly where to keep looking.

## Encounter Start
A creature that begins an encounter Hidden from every opponent is not detected until an observer succeeds a [[Skills|Perception]] Test against their Stealth DN, or the Hidden creature acts in a way that would reasonably reveal them (attacking, moving loudly, [[Movement Types|Running]], etc.). This falls naturally out of the rules above — undetected opponents have not declared [[Actions]] and can therefore only use a Reaction, if any, once revealed.

## Group Detection
Track Hidden separately per observer. A GM may simplify this for large crowds by rolling once for "the guards" as a group where individual tracking isn't meaningful to the scene.
