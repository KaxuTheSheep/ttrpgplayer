Characters advance using a single unified currency — **Trait Points** — from creation through the entire game. The same points spent at creation are the same points spent advancing later. There are no separate creation and advancement pools.

## Starting Points
At character creation, a character receives a starting pool of Trait Points, defined by the relevant module. This is referred to as **N**.

## Levelling Up
A character gains a further allotment of Trait Points, defined by the relevant module, each time they level up. This is referred to as **M**.

The relevant module defines what triggers a level up — narrative milestone, session count, XP threshold, or any other condition.

## Spending Points
Trait Points can be spent on:
- [[Attributes]]
- Skill training
- [[Sturdiness]] increases
- Discipline Nodes
- Any additional category defined by the relevant module (e.g. racial tree nodes, essence capacity)

## Banking Points
Unspent Trait Points carry forward indefinitely. There is no penalty for banking points across levels — some investments are expensive enough to reward planning ahead.
